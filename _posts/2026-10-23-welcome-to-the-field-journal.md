Hello and welcome to the new development tracker for Project Paleo.

Until now, updates have been scattered across short clips and playtest notes. I wanted a quiet, tidy place on the web that feels like the field journal from the game, somewhere you can check at any time without pop-ups, logins or trackers getting in the way.

## How This Site Works

Everything here is updated by me as I work on the game:

- **Dev Log:** Whenever I finish a feature, fix a tricky bug or put out a playtest build, I will log a new specimen note here. You can also follow along with an RSS reader.
- **Roadmap:** Drawn like geological rock strata. Planned features sit in the upper layers, what I am digging right now is in the middle trench, and finished systems rest in the settled bedrock at the bottom.
- **Builds:** A clear history of every open playtest build and what changed.
- **Art:** A live tally showing how many placeholder assets have been replaced with hand-made pixel art, UI, sound effects and music.
- **Gallery:** Screenshots and clips straight from recent builds.

Right now I am working on polishing the first two hours of the game based on your playtest feedback for version 0.17.0. Thank you for following the dig, and I hope you enjoy exploring the journal.
