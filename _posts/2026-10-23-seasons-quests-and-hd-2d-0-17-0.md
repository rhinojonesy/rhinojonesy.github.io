Build 0.17.0 is ready to play, and the world looks and feels much cosier than it did last week.

The biggest change you will notice straight away is the new HD-2D pixel look. Combining crisp pixel-art sprites with soft depth, warm sunlight and gentle shadows makes the rock ledges and country lanes feel like a miniature diorama you can step right into.

## New in Version 0.17.0

- **The four seasons:** You can now watch the town change through winter snow, warm autumn colours and spring blossom.
- **Quests from the townsfolk:** Your neighbours now have errands, requests and local stories to share.
- **Gold panning:** Take a pan down to the rivers and wash gravel to spot flakes and nuggets of gold.
- **A cottage with furniture and a garden:** You have a proper home base to decorate indoors and a garden patch to tend outside.
- **Eating and a calendar:** Keep track of the passing days and seasons, and stop for a bite to eat during long days in the field.
- **Fossil quality stars and colours:** Finds are now graded clearly with stars and colours, from Poor (rust) and Fair (muted) up through Good (cream), Excellent (gold) and Pristine (sea-glass).
- **Character and hair choices:** Choose a female or male character and pick from a selection of hair styles.
- **A bookshop in town:** Drop by the new bookshop to browse local reading.
- **Controller support:** You can now play comfortably with a controller on PC or on the Steam Deck.

Thank you to everyone who tried 0.16.2 and shared notes. Next up, I am focusing on polishing the opening two hours using your feedback and steadily replacing placeholder assets with hand-made art.
