Today is a big milestone for Project Paleo. Build 0.16.2 is out for our first open playtest, and people other than me are finally stepping off the bus and picking up a rock hammer.

I have been building this game on my own in Godot 4 for a good while now, so handing over the first two hours feels both exciting and a little nerve-wracking.

## What is in Build 0.16.2

This build focuses on the opening loop of the game so I can see how the core fossil-hunting feels in your hands:

- **Jo's dig:** You arrive in town by bus and meet your mentor, Jo, who walks you through spotting fossil beds and digging safely.
- **Brushing fossils out:** Once you lift a block of rock, you brush away the dust and shale to uncover the specimen underneath without damaging it.
- **The fossil journal:** Every real prehistoric species you find is logged in your field journal.
- **Field work and saving up:** You can take on early field work around town and put your earnings towards buying the plot for your own museum.

## What I am Looking Out For

Right now, plenty of sprites and sounds are still placeholder assets while I make sure the digging, brushing and journal menus feel clear and relaxing. If anything feels confusing or fiddly during those first two hours, please let me know. Every bit of feedback helps me shape the next update.
