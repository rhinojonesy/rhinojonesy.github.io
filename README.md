# Project Paleo Field Journal

This repository holds the development tracker website for **Project Paleo** (working title), a cosy fossil-hunting and museum-building game for PC, made in Godot 4 with an HD-2D pixel-art look and designed with the Steam Deck in mind.

## About the Game

In Project Paleo, you arrive by bus in a quiet country town and learn the basics of excavation from your mentor, Jo. You spend your days out in the field digging and carefully brushing fossils out of the rock, recording real prehistoric species in your field journal, and cleaning specimens in your prep lab.

As your collection grows, you build and organise your own town museum. Beyond your home town, a world of nine distinct regions opens up to explore by bus, train, ferry and plane. Between expeditions, you can pan for gold in the rivers, tend to your cottage and garden, follow the changing seasons and weather, and help the townsfolk with their quests.

## What This Site Tracks

- **Dev Log**: Regular notes from the solo developer on features, fixes, art, sound and playtests.
- **Roadmap**: Planned, active and completed milestones arranged like rock strata.
- **Playtest Builds**: Version history and highlights from each open playtest build.
- **Art Progress**: Progress on replacing placeholder assets with hand-made pixel art, UI, sound effects and music.
- **Gallery**: Screenshots and video clips directly from recent builds.
