Welcome to the dig

A cosy game about digging up the past

{{ site.pitch | escape }}

Arrive by bus in a small town, learn to dig from your mentor Jo, brush fossils out of the rock, fill a journal of real prehistoric species and build up your own museum. Explore nine regions by bus, train, ferry and plane, pan for gold in the rivers, decorate your cottage and garden, and help the townsfolk through every season.
