Field Manual

About Project Paleo

Project Paleo (working title) is a cosy fossil-hunting game for PC, made by a solo developer in Godot 4 with an HD-2D pixel-art look and with the Steam Deck in mind. It is made for people who love Stardew Valley and Animal Crossing.

Life in Town and Beyond

Your journey begins when you arrive by bus in a quiet country town with an old field bag and plenty of curiosity. Your mentor, Jo, teaches you how to read the ground, dig into fossil-bearing rock and gently brush fragile bones and shells free without chipping them.

Every specimen you unearth has a quality grade, from weathered Poor finds up to Fair, Good, Excellent and Pristine museum pieces. As you explore, you fill out an illustrated journal of real prehistoric species and save up for a plot to build and organise your own museum.

What You Can Do

  • Digging, brushing and the prep lab: Excavate fossils in the field and clean them carefully on your lab bench.
  • Build your own museum: Lay out display halls, arrange exhibits and share prehistoric history with the town.
  • Nine regions to explore: Travel across nine distinct regions by bus, train, ferry and plane, each with its own geology and fossil beds.
  • Cottage, garden and gold panning: Decorate your cosy cottage with furniture, tend a garden outside and pan for gold in the rivers.
  • Seasons, weather and townsfolk: Watch winter snow, autumn colours and spring blossom transform the landscape while taking on quests from your neighbours.
